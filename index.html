How We Work

Our Design Process

01

Discovery & Brief

We begin with a detailed site visit and client consultation to understand your lifestyle, requirements, budget, and vision for the space.

02

Concept & Design

We present 2D floor plans, 3D visualisations, material boards, and colour palettes — refined through your feedback until the design is exactly right.

03

Execution & Supervision

Our team manages vendors, site supervision, and quality checks through every stage — from civil work to final styling — ensuring zero compromise on quality.

04

Handover & After-care

We do a formal snag walk with you, address every item on the punch list, and provide after-care support for 6 months post-completion.
