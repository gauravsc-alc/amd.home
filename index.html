How We Work

Our Design Process

01

Discovery &amp; Brief

We begin with a detailed site visit and client consultation to understand your lifestyle, requirements, budget, and vision for the space.

02

Concept &amp; Design

We present 2D floor plans, 3D visualisations, material boards, and colour palettes — refined through your feedback until the design is exactly right.

03

Execution &amp; Supervision

Our team manages vendors, site supervision, and quality checks through every stage — from civil work to final styling.

04

Handover &amp; After-care

A formal snag walk, punch-list sign-off, and 6 months of after-care support to ensure every detail is perfect.

About Us

About AMD Home Interiors

We are a dedicated interior design studio specialising in bungalow design, terrace spaces, building elevations, pergola structures, and complete home interiors — living rooms, modular kitchens, and bedrooms.

Every project we take on reflects our belief that great design is rooted in the way a family actually lives — their rituals, their relationships, and their aspirations.

"A well-designed home is not just beautiful — it makes your everyday life easier, calmer, and more joyful."

What Makes Us Different

  • India-specific design sensibility — we understand Indian climates, lifestyles, and spatial constraints
  • Vastu-aware layouts integrated seamlessly with modern aesthetics
  • End-to-end supervision from concept to handover
  • Transparent pricing with no hidden costs
